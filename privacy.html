Privacy Policy

TYT Chat · Last updated October 2026

What TYT Chat is

TYT Chat is a desktop app that runs on the streamer's own Windows computer. It shows the streamer's Twitch and YouTube live chat in one place and lets them send messages and moderate their own chat. There is no TYT Chat server, website account or cloud service.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (through YouTube API Services, after the streamer signs in with Google): the streamer's channel name, their live broadcasts, and their live chat messages. It uses this to show chat, post messages the streamer types, and time out or ban users when the streamer chooses to.
  • Twitch (after the streamer signs in with Twitch): chat messages in the streamer's channel, and the moderation and channel actions the streamer chooses to run.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on the streamer's own computer, in the app's settings folder. Chat messages are kept in memory while the app is open and are not saved to disk. Nothing is sent to, or stored by, the developer of TYT Chat or any third party, other than the requests the app makes directly to YouTube and Twitch to do what the streamer asks.

Sharing

TYT Chat does not sell, share or transfer any user data. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Removing access

Click Sign out in the app's Settings to delete the stored sign-in and revoke it. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Twitch's Connections settings (twitch.tv/settings/connections). Uninstalling the app and deleting its settings folder removes all locally stored data.

Google and YouTube

TYT Chat uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
